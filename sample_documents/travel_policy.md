# Acme Global Corporate Travel & Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Overview & Principles
This policy sets forth the mandatory guidelines and spending limits for corporate travel, client entertainment, and business-related expense reimbursements at Acme Global.

## 2. Employee Level Bands
For the purpose of business travel benefits and expenditure caps, employees are mapped to the following level bands:
- **Band L1 - L2**: Associate & Professional Staff
- **Band L3**: Senior Staff & Team Leads
- **Band L4**: Directors, Vice Presidents & Executive Leadership

## 3. Lodging & Hotel Allowances
Nightly room rates (excluding local taxes) must strictly remain within the prescribed caps:

| Employee Band | Domestic Lodging Limit (Nightly) | Annual Maximum Lodging Allowance | International Lodging (Nightly) |
|---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| L1 - L2 | $175 | $1,200 | $250 |
| **L3** | **$250** | **$2,000** | **$350** |
| L4 | $400 | $3,500 | $550 |

*Note: For high-cost metropolitan tier-1 cities (San Francisco, New York, London, Tokyo), nightly caps automatically increase by 25%.*

## 4. Meals & Incidentals (Per Diem)
- L1 - L3 employees receive a daily per diem rate of $75 for domestic trips and $110 for international travel.
- L4 executive employees receive a daily per diem rate of $120 domestic and $160 international.
- Alcohol may only be expensed during official client entertainment dinners and requires itemized receipts.

## 5. Expense Submission & Invoice Formatting
- **Submission Deadline**: All reimbursement claims must be submitted to Finance within **30 days** of incurring the business expense or concluding the travel itinerary. Claims submitted after 60 days are subject to rejection.
- **Invoice & Receipt Format**: Expenses must reference an official vendor receipt or invoice. The internal tracking code format follows `INV-YYYY-XXX` (e.g., `INV-2026-042`).
- **Required Documentation**:
  1. Itemized receipt showing vendor name, date, and description of service/items.
  2. Brief business justification (e.g., "Client QBR on-site visit").
  3. Manager sign-off for expenses exceeding $500.
