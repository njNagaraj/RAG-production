# Acme Global Employee Handbook & Workplace Guidelines

## 1. Welcome to Acme Global
Welcome to Acme Global! This handbook outlines the policies, benefits, and workplace standards that govern employment at Acme Global. All employees are expected to read and adhere to these guidelines.

## 2. Employment Categories & Probation
- **Probationary Period**: All newly hired employees undergo a probationary evaluation period of exactly **90 days** from their start date.
- **Confirmation**: Upon satisfactory completion of probation, employment status is confirmed in writing by People Operations.
- **Notice Period**: During the probationary period, the required notice period for termination or resignation is 14 days. Following confirmation, the standard notice period is **60 days**.

## 3. Leave & Paid Time Off (PTO)
Acme Global believes in work-life harmony and provides comprehensive leave benefits:

### 3.1 Annual Vacation Leave
- Full-time confirmed employees accrue **25 days** of paid annual vacation leave per calendar year.
- A maximum of 10 unused vacation days may be carried forward into the subsequent calendar year.

### 3.2 Casual & Sick Leave
- Employees receive **12 days** of casual leave annually for personal matters or short illnesses.
- Consecutive absences exceeding 3 business days require a registered medical practitioner's certificate.

### 3.3 Parental Leave
- **Maternity Leave**: Eligible female employees are entitled to **26 weeks** of fully paid maternity leave for their first two children, and 12 weeks for subsequent children.
- **Paternity Leave**: Biological or adoptive secondary caregivers receive **4 weeks** of fully paid paternity leave to be taken within the first 6 months of the child's arrival.

## 4. Employee Health & Wellness Benefits
- **Comprehensive Healthcare**: Acme Global covers 100% of employee health insurance premiums and 80% for qualified dependents.
- **Wellness Allowance**: All full-time employees are eligible for an annual **$1,200 wellness allowance** to reimburse gym memberships, mental health apps, ergonomic equipment, and fitness classes.
- **Mental Health Assistance**: Free, confidential counseling is available 24/7 through our Employee Assistance Program (EAP).

## 5. Working Hours & Hybrid Work
- Standard working hours are 40 hours per week, with core collaboration hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM local time.
- Hybrid employees are expected to be present in their designated office 2 days per week, with flexible remote work for the remaining days.
