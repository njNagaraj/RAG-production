# Acme Global Employee Benefits & Perks Guide

## 1. Eligibility Criteria
All permanent, regular full-time employees working 30 or more hours per week are eligible for company-sponsored benefits starting on the first day of the calendar month following their date of hire. Part-time employees (20-29 hours/week) are eligible for pro-rated benefits.

## 2. Health & Dental Coverage
- **Medical Insurance**: Choice between High Deductible Health Plan (HDHP with HSA contribution) and Preferred Provider Organization (PPO).
- **Dental & Vision**: 100% preventive coverage including bi-annual cleanings and annual optical exams, plus $200 annual hardware allowance for prescription glasses or contact lenses.

## 3. Wellness Allowances & Physical Well-being
- Every full-time confirmed employee is eligible to receive an annual **$1,200 wellness allowance**.
- Eligible reimbursable items include:
  - Fitness club and gym memberships
  - Marathon, triathlon, and athletic race entry fees
  - Ergonomic home office furniture (standing desks, ergonomic chairs)
  - Meditation and mental wellness mobile subscriptions
  - Physical therapy and preventative sports massage sessions

## 4. Professional Development & Learning Stipend
- Employees receive an annual education stipend of up to **$2,500** for approved technical conferences, professional certifications, books, and accredited course tuition.
- A 6-month post-completion retention commitment applies to training programs exceeding $1,000.

## 5. Retirement & 401(k) Matching
Acme Global matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to the first 4% of base salary, and 50% on the next 2%, with immediate 100% vesting from day one.
